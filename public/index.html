Making a trailer

        
    
          
  1. Add your recordings, screenshots and a music track (drop them anywhere).
    2. 
          
  2. Pick a clip, drag the in / out handles or press I / O; S splits a long recording at the playhead.
    3. 
          
  3. Drag clips to reorder. Click the marker between two clips to pick a transition.
    4. 
          
  4. Click the music bar under the clips for its volume and fades; Whole video on the right has the fade in / out and loudness.
    5. 
          
  5. P renders a quick draft and plays it. Then choose Steam (or a Discord size) and press Export.
    6. 
        

        
A single clip with nothing applied and the No limit preset is copied without re-encoding (instant, keyframe accurate). Everything else is re-encoded frame accurately.

      

      

        
Shortcuts

        

          
SpacePlay / pause

          
I / OSet in / out point

          
← / →Step one frame (Shift: one second)

          
Home / EndJump to in / out point

          
SSplit clip at playhead

          
DDuplicate clip

          
DeleteRemove the selected clip, transition or music

          
, / .Previous / next clip

          
Alt+← / →Move clip earlier / later

          
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo / redo

          
PRender and play the draft preview

          
EnterExport

          
EscCancel export / close

          
Ctrl+SSave project
